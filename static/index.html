How to use VU Mixer

  1. Pick your microphone in the MIC strip. Its gain and VU meter are shown there. Use the A/B buttons below it to choose which output buses receive the mic — e.g. turn A off so you don't hear yourself, keep B on to send the voice to the other PC over HDMI. The vol → A / vol → B sliders set how loud the mic is inside each bus, so you can boost the mic for one output (up to 200%) without changing the other.
  2. Noise suppression: press NS on the mic strip to remove background noise with the built-in WebRTC filter (no extra software installed).
  3. Pick a device for A and for B — for example your headset on A and HDMI on B. Desktop audio is copied to both; the mic only to the buses you enabled.
  4. Desktop audio is captured by making VM-System the default output. If something is already playing elsewhere, press Catch desktop audio.
  5. Other apps (Discord, OBS, browsers…) should select Monitor of VM-Mic as their input. Then they get the same mixed mic signal without grabbing the hardware microphone.
  6. Audio flows only while this app is running. Closing it removes the virtual devices and restores your previous defaults.

Tip: this is the Linux/PipeWire equivalent of VoiceMeeter. All routing happens natively in PipeWire — no audio passes through the browser or Python.
